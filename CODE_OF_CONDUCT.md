# Code of Conduct

Project spaces should be welcoming, useful, and safe for people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Participate with patience, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and respect boundaries and privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sustained personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a non-public contact listed on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible.

This code applies in project-managed spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
